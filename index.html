Why We're Building This

The Concept

The DMV has great golfers and not enough great places for them to practice. Capital Golf Labs is fixing that — a quiet, private environment built entirely around serious improvement.

Tour-Level Technology

Powered by Trackman iO - the gold standard in launch monitor technology. Trusted by tour professionals worldwide to measure every detail of your game with unmatched precision.

24/7 Smartphone Access

Train entirely on your schedule. Book from your phone, approach the secure door reader, and unlock the facility using your encrypted digital Bluetooth credential. Full training autonomy with zero staff, zero check-in lines, and zero distractions.

Member Benefits

Membership goes beyond bay access. Members receive a personalized Trackman performance profile, exclusive equipment access through our PXG partnership, and priority access to founding rates.

Tour-Level Technology.
Available to You.

The Trackman iO is mounted overhead and captures every shot with radar-synchronized high-speed video. No floor pads, no interference, no guesswork.

Every session generates a precise data record: ball speed, club speed, spin rate, launch angle, carry distance, and impact video. This is how tour players practice. Now it's yours.

  • Overhead radar + radar-synchronized impact camera
  • Full swing, short game, and putting data
  • Shot dispersion mapping across every club
  • Session history saved to your Trackman profile
