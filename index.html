The Concept

A New Way to Practice and Play

A private Trackman golf simulator studio that's yours whenever you want it. No front desk, no crowds, no waiting on a bay.

  1. Step 01

    Book

    Reserve a time from your phone, day or night.

  2. Step 02

    Let yourself in

    Every booking comes with its own door code.

  3. Step 03

    Play

    The bay and lounge are yours and your guest's. Every shot is saved to your Trackman profile.

By Design

Membership is capped on purpose.

One bay and a limited number of members, so there's a time open when you want to play.
