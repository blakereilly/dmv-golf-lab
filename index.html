02 How It Works

From Your Phone to Your First Shot

No front desk, no check-in, no waiting. Three steps and the bay is yours.

  1. Step 01

    Book From Your Phone

    Reserve a bay 24/7 in one-hour blocks. Pick your time — it's yours.

  2. Step 02

    Unlock at the Door

    Every booking gets its own door code. Walk up, enter it, and you're in. No keys, no staff, no line.

  3. Step 03

    Hit & Review Your Data

    Warm up, play a course, or grind your numbers — every shot saved to your Trackman profile.
