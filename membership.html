Studio Rules & Booking Policies

Reservation Rules: Bookings are made in 1-hour increments. To prevent scheduling abuse, members may hold a maximum of 2 active reservations on the schedule concurrently. As soon as a session is completed, another slot can be immediately reserved.

No Long-Term Contracts: All memberships run on a standard month-to-month cycle. There are zero signup fees, termination penalties, or hidden costs. Cancel or change your tier at any time directly through your account dashboard.
