Good to Know

How the Studio Works

Booking

Reserve in one-hour blocks, 24/7, from your phone. You can hold up to two reservations at a time; the moment a session ends, you're free to book another.

Guests & Occupancy

Every session includes one guest, free. Want to make it a foursome? Add up to two more guests for $15 each. Four players max per bay.

Cancellations

Plans change — just give us four hours' notice and there's no charge. Cancellations inside four hours, or a no-show, are a flat $25.

A Focused Space

Your bay is private to your party — no walk-ins, no crowds. It's a practice studio, not a bar: no alcohol, no distractions, just you and your data.

No Contracts

Every plan is month-to-month. No signup fee, no cancellation penalty, no fine print. Move between Signature and Elite anytime from your account.

Founding Rates

Join during the founding window and your rate is locked for as long as you stay a member, even after standard pricing goes up.
