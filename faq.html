Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Everything worth knowing before your first session. Still have a question? Email us and we'll get right back to you.

When do you open, and where?

We're in buildout now at Fallfax Center — 2830-B Fallfax Drive, Falls Church, VA — targeting Fall 2026. Join the waitlist and you'll be first in when founding memberships open.

How do I get in if there's no staff?

You book from your phone, walk up, and unlock the door with your own encrypted mobile credential. No keys, no front desk, no waiting — for your session, the bay is yours.

Is it safe to practice alone, late at night?

Yes. The studio is monitored by security cameras throughout, every entry is tied to your personal credential, and support is reachable by phone or text if anything ever comes up.

Do I bring my own clubs?

Bring your own — this is your practice, dialed to your gear. If you're between sets, our PXG Fairfax partnership is a great place to get fit and build one.

What should I wear?

Athletic clothes and clean, non-marking athletic or golf shoes. Metal spikes aren't allowed on the turf.

Can I bring a guest?

Every session includes one guest, free. Want to make it a foursome? Add up to two more for an additional fee — four players max per bay.

What can I work on?

Everything. Full swing, short game, and putting, plus Trackman's course library, driving ranges, and skills challenges. Every shot is captured to your personal Trackman profile.
