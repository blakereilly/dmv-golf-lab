Good to Know

Frequently Asked.

Everything worth knowing before your first session. Still have a question? Email us and we'll get right back to you.

01Where are you located?

We're open now, 24/7, at Fallfax Center — 2830-B Fallfax Drive, Falls Church, VA.

02Where do I park?

Parking is free and there's usually plenty of it. There's also a reserved member spot in front of the blue garage door.

03How do I get in if there's no staff?

Every booking comes with its own unique door code. Book from your phone, walk up, enter your code, and you're in. No keys, no front desk, no waiting — for your session, the bay is yours.

04Is it safe to practice alone, late at night?

Yes. The studio is monitored by security cameras throughout, every door code is tied to a specific booking, and support is reachable by phone or text at (301) 580-6496 if anything ever comes up.

05Do I bring my own clubs?

For the best experience, bring the clubs you play with. We also keep a few house clubs on hand. Reach out for more information.

06Does the bay work for left-handed golfers?

Yes. There's plenty of room for both left- and right-handed players.

07What should I wear?

Athletic clothes and clean, non-marking athletic or golf shoes. Metal spikes aren't allowed on the turf.

08Can I bring food and drinks?

Yes. Outside food and drink are welcome, as long as you clean up after yourself. There's also free water and seltzer in the fridge.

09Can I bring a guest?

Yes. Every session includes one guest, free.

10What can I work on?

Everything. Full swing, short game, and putting, plus Trackman's course library, driving ranges, and skills challenges. Every shot is captured to your personal Trackman profile.

11How do I join?

Two options: the Signature membership is $225/month with 5 hours included, month-to-month. The Session Pack is a one-time $165 for 3 one-hour bookings you can use anytime within 3 months. See both →

12Can I try it before I join?

Yes. Come check out the studio with a free session first. Text us at (301) 580-6496 or email us and we'll get you set up.

13How does booking work once I join?

Book anytime through the Trackman Golf app or the Trackman Booking web portal. After you sign up, we'll send you everything you need to get set up and book your first session.

14How far ahead can I book, and for how long?

Signature members can book up to 14 days ahead, hold up to 2 hours of upcoming bookings at a time, and book sessions of up to 2 hours. Session Pack holders can book up to 7 days ahead, hold 1 hour at a time, and book 1-hour sessions.

15Will I be able to get a time with only one bay?

Yes. Membership is capped on purpose. We limit the number of members so the bay isn't overbooked and you can get a time when you want to play.

16Do unused hours roll over?

No — your 5 included hours are use-them-or-lose-them each month. Need more time? Additional hours are $40/hr.

Ready to lock in your spot?

Memberships are open. Want to see the studio first? Text us for a free session.
